# Ollin — Movement

## Quick reference

- **Position:** 17 of 20
- **Literal translation:** Movement
- **Glyph description:** A distinctive interlaced or four-lobed movement sign.
- **Day-sign deity listed by AztecCalendar.com:** Xolotl

## Literal meaning and glyph

*Ollin* means **movement**. It should not automatically be translated as “earthquake,” although earthquakes can be one manifestation of movement.

## Core meanings and symbolism

Ollin means movement and names dynamic change. Nahui Ollin (“4 Movement”) names the present cosmic era; the Sun Stone’s program associates the sign with the four preceding eras and a dynamic cosmos [S2]. Earthquake can be one manifestation, but does not exhaust its meaning.

## Cultural and historical context

## Day-sign deity association

AztecCalendar.com lists **Xolotl** as associated with the Ollin day sign. This is a source-specific calendrical mapping, not a claim that every manuscript or scholar uses the same assignment. Keep it distinct from the deity associated with a **day numeral** and the patron of a **13-day trecena**; a full date can involve all these layers.

## App-safe description

> **Ollin means “Movement.” The sign is especially important because the present cosmic era was known as Nahui Ollin, “4 Movement.” On monuments such as the Sun Stone, Ollin expresses the dynamic and changing condition of the cosmos rather than merely an earthquake.**
