# Ehecatl — Wind

## Quick reference

- **Position:** 2 of 20
- **Literal translation:** Wind
- **Glyph description:** A wind-associated head or face, often recalling the characteristic beaked mask of Ehecatl-Quetzalcoatl.
- **Day-sign deity listed by AztecCalendar.com:** Quetzalcoatl

## Literal meaning and glyph

*Ehēcatl* means **wind**. In central Mexican religion, wind was not merely weather: it was an active force that moved clouds, announced rain, swept and opened space, and set cosmic processes into motion.

## Core meanings and symbolism

Ehecatl evokes an invisible force that moves through and changes the world [S2, S4]. Wind can suggest breath, clearing, the movement of clouds and rain, and cosmic motion in Ehecatl-Quetzalcoatl traditions [S1, S2]. Breath is a broader association, not the glyph’s literal meaning.

## Cultural and historical context

The divine form **Ehecatl-Quetzalcoatl** was closely associated with wind. The wind god’s projecting mask or beak is among the most distinctive iconographic features in central Mexican art. In creation traditions, Quetzalcoatl also participates in setting the present world in motion and in bringing life to humanity.

Wind therefore evokes **movement, breath, invisible force, and the opening of pathways**. These are broader cultural associations, not a fixed horoscope definition.

## Day-sign deity association

AztecCalendar.com lists **Quetzalcoatl** as associated with the Ehecatl day sign. This is a source-specific calendrical mapping, not a claim that every manuscript or scholar uses the same assignment. Keep it distinct from the deity associated with a **day numeral** and the patron of a **13-day trecena**; a full date can involve all these layers.

## App-safe description

> **Ehecatl means “Wind.” Its imagery is connected with Ehecatl-Quetzalcoatl and with wind as an invisible force that moves through the world, clears paths, carries clouds, and participates in cosmic motion.**

## Editorial cautions

**Avoid:** equating Ehecatl with “free spirit” or another modern personality archetype unless sourced.
