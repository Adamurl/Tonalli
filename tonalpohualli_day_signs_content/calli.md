# Calli — House

## Quick reference

- **Position:** 3 of 20
- **Literal translation:** House
- **Glyph description:** A house or architectural structure.
- **Day-sign deity listed by AztecCalendar.com:** Tepeyollotl

## Literal meaning and glyph

*Calli* literally means **house**. The glyph represents built, inhabited space: a place enclosed from the outside world.

## Core meanings and symbolism

The house marks protected, inhabited space. It can evoke household continuity, family, inheritance, domestic ritual, work, production, and the household as a basic social unit. Calli also participates in naming years as one of four year-bearer signs [S2, S6].

## Cultural and historical context

For Nahua communities the house was not merely a private residence. It was a fundamental social unit tied to **family, household ritual, inheritance, production, lineage, and community organization**. House imagery therefore marks the human-made and inhabited world.

Calli is especially important because it is one of the **four year-bearer signs** used in naming the 52 years of the calendrical cycle, along with Tochtli, Acatl, and Tecpatl.

## Day-sign deity association

AztecCalendar.com lists **Tepeyollotl** as associated with the Calli day sign. This is a source-specific calendrical mapping, not a claim that every manuscript or scholar uses the same assignment. Keep it distinct from the deity associated with a **day numeral** and the patron of a **13-day trecena**; a full date can involve all these layers.
