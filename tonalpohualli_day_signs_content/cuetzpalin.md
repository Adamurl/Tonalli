# Cuetzpalin — Lizard

## Quick reference

- **Position:** 4 of 20
- **Literal translation:** Lizard
- **Glyph description:** A small lizard with an elongated body and tail.
- **Day-sign deity listed by AztecCalendar.com:** Huehuecoyotl

## Literal meaning and glyph

*Cuetzpalin* is the **lizard**. The historical sources sometimes explain auguries by referring directly to observed animal behavior.

## Core meanings and symbolism

Sahagún’s 1-Lizard passage compares bodily toughness and recovery with a lizard surviving a fall and quickly running away [S1]. This specific trecena interpretation draws on animal observation; it should not be generalized into a fixed meaning for every Cuetzpalin day.

## Cultural and historical context

Sahagún’s discussion of the trecena **Ce Cuetzpalin (1 Lizard)** says that people born in that sign were compared to the lizard’s physical toughness: a lizard could fall and immediately run away unharmed. The text associates the trecena with bodily vigor, diligence, and work.

This is a good example of how Nahua divination could draw meaning from **observation and analogy with the natural world**.

Those passages describe **Ce Cuetzpalin and its trecena**, not a universal personality definition for every date containing the Cuetzpalin glyph.

## Day-sign deity association

AztecCalendar.com lists **Huehuecoyotl** as associated with the Cuetzpalin day sign. This is a source-specific calendrical mapping, not a claim that every manuscript or scholar uses the same assignment. Keep it distinct from the deity associated with a **day numeral** and the patron of a **13-day trecena**; a full date can involve all these layers.
