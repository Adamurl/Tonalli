Both English sources are retained separately. Their page numbers, wording, and calendrical associations belong to their respective source records.

| Source | English content | Organization |

| New photographed source | [Website content index](new-source/README.md) | Separate records for all twenty day signs, thirteen numbers, and the broader chapters. Each includes section notes, full translated text, and printed-page references. |

The [page-by-page translation of the new source](translation.en.md) is the unchanged baseline for its organized records. [New-source translation notes](SOURCE_NOTES.md) document gaps, uncertain readings, and source discrepancies.

## Using the organized records

Each record has a stable key, source identifier, entity type, and printed-page list in its Markdown frontmatter. Its section-notes table identifies potential webpage sections and links to their translated passages by printed page.

The notes describe what each passage covers. The full English translation remains separate and retains the source's own writing and claims. Associations from the earlier reference have not been used to fill gaps in the new source.

The broader records cover background to the numbers, calendar periods, year companions, veintenas, companion tables, trecenas, the source diagram, nature and the directions, and astronomy. These passages have their own subject headings rather than being folded into a day sign or numeral.

## Retained text and removed media

Both English source records are authored content and remain available during media cleanup. The original photos and PDF scans remain removed. PDF links inside the unchanged earlier reference therefore identify historical source-file locations, not available local documents. The existing sign-art links in that reference still point to the project's glyph assets.

The files are prepared for website integration. They have not been imported into Supabase or connected to the frontend.
