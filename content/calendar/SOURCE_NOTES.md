# Calendar translation source notes

The new source's page-by-page text is [translation.en.md](translation.en.md). It contains the English translation of 80 distinct photographed pages, organized by subject and printed page. The text is unchanged from the checked HEIC translation, including its quotations, captions, tables, source spellings, and uncertainty markers. Names and Nahuatl technical terms remain in their source forms. The [website content index](new-source/README.md) links to full translations grouped by symbol, number, and broader subject, with section notes and printed-page references.

The book's statements and its own interpretations are translated as written. No new interpretive copy has been added. Source numerical, historical, scientific, linguistic, and character claims were not corrected or independently established by translation. The supplied photographs did not identify the edition's full publication details.

## Coverage

- Input: 84 HEIC photographs, representing 80 distinct main printed pages.
- Range: printed pages 115–201.
- Missing complete pages: 138, 141, 174, 175, 176, 178, and 188.
- Duplicate photographed pages: 116, 117, 121, and 151. Each appears once in the translation.
- Page 159's number is inferred from its position between pages 158 and 160 because its photographed footer was obscured.
- Clipped facing-page strips did not provide recoverable continuous text for the missing pages. A strip of page 114 was also insufficient for translation.

All main pages were visually checked during translation. An independent check compared every table cell, numerical value, caption, and surrounding text on pages 143, 169–171, 173, 177, 190, 200, and 201. No proven omission or mistranslation was found in that checked scope.

The original photos, converted images, PDF scans, OCR output, per-photo duplicate translations, extraction tools, and the superseded display-copy draft were removed during cleanup. The earlier English reference was also removed in error and has been restored unchanged at [its original path](../../books/ENGLISH_TRANSLATIONS_AND_SYMBOL_REFERENCE.md). It is a separate source record. Image filenames in the new translation's HTML comments are provenance identifiers, not file links.

## Source distinctions preserved for website use

- Page 143's table includes the printed spellings `Chuicnahui` and `Uauantli`. Names that vary across captions, prose, or tables are not automatically different figures.
- Pages 144 and 168 explicitly distinguish `Tlazohteotl` from `Tlazolteotl`. Page 182 also uses these different forms in its caption and drawing.
- Page 156 uses `Uauantli`, `Huahuantli`, and `Uahuantli`. Page 164 includes `Mahtlactli Uan Ome`, `Quetzacoatl`, and `Tlahizcalpantecuhtli`. Page 165's lower label spells `Xiochiquetzalli`. These source variants remain in the translation.
- Page 167's period table preserves the source values: 7 / 52 years, 10 / 360 days, 20 / 20 days, 20 / 260 days, 13 / 13 days, and 9 / 9 days.
- Pages 169–170 preserve all 52 year-and-companion pairs. Page 171 preserves all 18 veintena pairs, including the abbreviations `Huitzilo-Tezcatli.` and `Tlaloc-Chalchiuh`.
- Page 173's day-sign table assigns `Chalchiuhtlicue` to Tecpatl. Its trecena table pairs `Ixcuinan` and `Tlazohteotl` with Ce Ollin, while the later prose discusses Tezcatlipoca as a bird. These statements were not changed to make them agree.
- Page 177 preserves all 13 rows of numeral, day lord, flying being, and further companion.
- Page 187 preserves both `Tlalchitonatiuh` and `Tlachitonatiuh` as printed.
- Page 190's diagram names are grouped by position above, left, right, and below the central illustration. Rows across columns do not establish associations between names.
- Page 195 prints only four trecenas under Huitzilanpa. No fifth was supplied.
- Pages 199–201 preserve all astronomical values and arithmetic claims. Page 201 visibly prints `5884.4` in one division statement, despite adjacent statements using `584.4`. This source error is retained.

## Content boundaries

The translation's sections cover day signs; numbers and their companions; calendar companion tables; trecenas; the summary of calendar companions; additional writings on nature, the dual principle, and directions; and astronomy.

Page boundaries and mid-sentence continuations remain intact. The missing page 188 leaves a gap in the trecena discussion. Page 189 ends after the opening reading of the twentieth trecena; page 190 is a diagram, so no continuation was invented.

Uncaptioned illustrations were not replaced with invented explanations. The translation is a local content source and has not been imported into Supabase or connected to the website's rendered sections.
