### Printed page 171

#### Table of the companions of the 18 veintenas

Hieroglyphs of the 18 veintenas in *The Pre-Hispanic Calendars* by Alfonso Caso.

#### Names of the veintenas and their companions

| Position | Months | Rulers |

| 2 | Tlacaxipehualiztli | Xipe Totec |

In the table of hieroglyphs of the twenty-day months taken from Alfonso Caso's work, we observe that the representative drawing for Atlacahualo also has a tree, because one of the other names by which this month is known is Cuahuitlehua, the trees are raised. In the place corresponding to Tlaxochimaco is the bundle of a dead person, giving the other name of this veintena, Miccailhuitontli, the Feast of the Small Dead. In the following month, corresponding to Xocotl huetzi, a similar change occurs, giving the reading Hueymicailhuitl, the Feast of the Great Dead or the Great Feast of the Dead.

### Printed page 172

For the following illustration we take the drawings of the veintenas from the wheel of months and years on plate three of the calendars of the Chavero Collection, where there are some variations from the preceding table. For example, Atlacahualo is represented in its respective hieroglyph by a tender ear of maize, giving the name Xilomaniztli, offering of young ears of maize, and Teotleco and Tepeilhuitl are represented by heads covered with hay, pachtli, because of the names Pachtontli and Hueypachtli by which these veintenas are also known in the sources.
