## Full English translation

### Printed page 169

After these examples, necessary for introducing us to the descriptive field of the tonalamatl, we shall begin by listing the companions of years, months, trecenas, days, and once again numbers, so that the entire record is brought together in this part of the work, highlighting the first series of each list in bold.

After the list of the rulers of the years according to the Borbonicus Codex, next in importance is the influence recorded in each group of twenty days. Let us remember that the eighteen months reflect in their names the conditions prevailing during the year, and the accompanying element is appropriate to each of them. The Sun as Huitzilopochtli and Tlaloc as the liquor of the Earth are present in nature more often than any of the others.
