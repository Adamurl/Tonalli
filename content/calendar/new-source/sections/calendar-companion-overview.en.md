## Full English translation

### Printed page 190

- Companions of the years.
- Companions of the twenty-day periods.
- Companions of the trecenas.
- Companions of the twenty signs.
- The thirteen Lords of the days.
- The thirteen bird companions of the numbers.
- The companions of the birds that emerge from their beaks.
- The nine Lords of the night.
