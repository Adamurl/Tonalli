## Full English translation

### Printed page 191

They all regulate everything that exists through the two important branches of ancient science, Tezcatlipoca and Quetzalcoatl, as well as the elements that act in either branch, such as Xiuhtecuhtli and the lords of Mictlan.

Quetzalcoatl, the Beautiful Serpent, is the vital essence, the essence of life.

Tezcatlipoca is the great universal consciousness and the inner development of human beings. Complementing one another, these two forms of vital essence are present in every corner of the world and every corner of the universe comprehensible to the human mind. They are the two enormous serpents that frame all the essence of life on the Stone of the Suns.

How are we involved with that essence?

What is the essence of life that we call energy without specifying what kind of energy it is?

#### The essence of life from a cosmic principle and from the four directions of the world

Everything is dual energy; it manifests in our thoughts, ideas, attitudes, and emotions in the psychic sphere, and is physically implicit in heat, sound, light, colors, electricity, and the magnetism given off by bodies under certain conditions. We are surrounded by emanations of various energies generated by the elements of nature, by everything that has life, and by what is apparently inanimate. From the four directions of the universe and the four corners of the world, these emanations of dual essence nourish our tonalli; we are penetrated by them. We generate and emit dual energy in our movements, in our words, and even in our intentions. We relate to everything that exists in our environment through vibrations or emanations of that dual nature; therefore, we are psychic essence in Tezcatlipoca and we are essence of life with the Quetzalcoatl that is implicit within us from the beginning of our formation and fully manifests from the moment of our birth.

At present, conscious acceptance that we are generators and possess our own psychic and vital essences, and the handling of them, is not taught to us anywhere. Despite being extremely important, knowledge of them is not included in any learning program.

### Printed page 192

And it is perhaps more important than learning to read and write, or adopting good health or behavioral habits! Through calendrical mathematics we realize that our ancestors knew these essences, used them, and recorded them in the personification of the elements of nature and in the behavioral relationship of human beings with their influence.

In antiquity, the strength emanating from Tonantzintlalli, the Earth, was also systematically used.

The most important pre-Hispanic buildings were constructed over vortices of helical energy that springs forth naturally or as a function of certain astronomical alignments on dates when the zenith passages of the Sun and Moon and of stellar groups over these points, predetermined in antiquity, stand out.

All concepts concerning the elements and their importance to human beings refer to knowledge and the usefulness of handling the different types of psychic or vital force that we receive from them and that our being generates and gives to the beings and things around us.

We receive the tonalli of the Sun, rain, trees, mountains, Earth, air, Moon, animals, and the people who cross our paths each day in everyday life, in the form of affection, heat, moisture, words, songs, and whispers, nourishing our own tonalli in the thirteen Lords of the day and our nahualli contained in the tonalamatl, the nine Lords of the night.

The concept of dual essence is something real and present in our lives. We believe it is important to become increasingly aware of the need to know it through calendrical mathematics, to know what potential we have stored from the moment of birth and, consequently, to learn to use it with the full responsibility of elevating our human condition, never degrading it. It is not something we have to invent or strive to discover; it is simply allowing the Quetzalcoatl and the Tezcatlipoca that we all carry within to become present in our lives, and, as Mexicans, accepting that it is part of our ancestors' knowledge, still alive in Indigenous communities that have not lost all their traditional formation.

Let them flow, let them manifest in such a way that we can activate them as an integral part of our own being; remember that the essence or energy of each of them is already within us. We only have to be willing to feel it, recognize it, and experience it. Experiencing Tezcatlipoca and Quetzalcoatl through their many manifestations is to feel alive; life is a joyful and comforting act when lived fully.

### Printed page 193

It is necessary to learn to live each moment enjoying the true sensation of being alive, receiving and sharing the dual essence that surrounds us, for life is a constant manifestation of that primordial essence.

It is an ancestral idea, common to many ancient peoples, that an invisible force animates living beings. Unfortunately, among their successors the idea has remained a religious, esoteric, or magical proposition.

From the Indigenous viewpoint in pre-Hispanic antiquity, reading between the lines of the sparseness or distortion of the sources concerning the analysis of deities, but comparing the study with present-day aspects of the phenomenon known as nagualism, it is inferred that there is, in fact, dual essence in all things, living or nonliving. These allow constant renewal or transmutation through understanding what the elements of nature and the components of all creation truly are for human beings.

At present there are different viewpoints concerning the nature of dual essence, because attempts have been made to verify it through scientific rigor, discovering that it manifests physically and mentally through chemical, electrical, and electromagnetic processes, but vital energy is something more than this.

We live in a universe of dual essence, in states graded from the most subtle to the most solid. These manifestations depend on the frequency at which that essence or energy vibrates: the lower the frequency, the greater the density; the higher the frequency, the greater the subtlety.

Mirror of the Tezcatlipoca.

Primordial unity in the generating Principle of Duality.

Emblem of the conch shell worn by the Quetzalcoatl.

There is a Dual Generating Principle from which it comes; from it it arises and to it it returns, making it an ever-changing and fluid phenomenon. Let us accept, from our present way of thinking, that this principle is located in the nucleus of every atom, every cell

and every molecule in everything that exists in creation, in constant interaction, generating and emitting and at the same time attracting and absorbing dual essence from everything that exists. This is symbolized in the spiral of the conch shell of the Quetzalcoatl, just as psychic and spiritual aspects are contained in the representation of the smoke of Tezcatlipoca's obsidian mirror.

The ancient Toltecs called this Principle Ometeotl.

Just as this Principle manifests in the Whole, it is also in each living being; it exists above and below and is outside and inside us. It is important to recognize that before we were beings, we were soul, spirit, essence, and that this essence becomes present beyond the limits of our physical being. The essence emerges and expands outward beyond our skin.

In this way we interrelate without limiting ourselves to the physical realm, but in an energetic context, without needing to touch other beings or the things around us physically.

We fill ourselves with dual essence when we are in contact with nature, with our bare feet touching the earth or plants, or through the nearness of animals, especially those very close to human beings, receiving it in these many forms from the four directions. Likewise, other people with positive thoughts and attitudes shape a pleasant tonalli in us through their dealings with us, as we feel good beside them.

At present we can compare what is understood by the general term energy with the concept of Ometeotl through the opinion of psychotherapist Ana Luisa Martínez Sánchez, from whom we have largely taken the preceding reflections.

In the tonalpohualli, each of the 260 dates that compose it is linked to one of the four sacred directions of the world in the first eight plates of the Cospi, Vaticanus B, and Borgia codices.

This link between human beings whose birth date is recorded in the tonalamatl and each of the directions can be observed:

The trecenas that begin with:

- One Crocodilian, Ce cipactli
- One Reed, Ce acatl
- One Serpent, Ce coatl
- One Movement, Ce ollin
- One Water, Ce atl

And all the days recorded within them are dedicated to the direction where light is born, called Tlahuiztlanpa, the east, granting luminosity and confidence to emerge with determination whenever one falls into darkness. In their daily lives they radiate security and kindness.

The trecenas that begin with:

- One Jaguar, Ce ocelotl
- One Death, Ce miquiztli
- One Flint, Ce tecpatl
- One Wind, Ce ehecatl

Belong to the direction surrounded by thorns, Huitzilanpa, metaphorically called the place of wisdom acquired through discipline and deprivation, granting the quality of knowing how to choose only what is transcendent and valuable.

The trecenas that begin with:

- One Deer, Ce mazatl
- One Rain, Ce quiahuitl
- One Monkey, Ce ozomahtli
- One House, Ce calli
- One Eagle, Ce cuauhtli

Together with all the days that compose them, belong to Cihuatlanpa, the place of feminine essence, granting qualities of intuition, a sense of order, and administrative ability, as well as great sensitivity.

The trecenas that begin with:

- One Flower, Ce xochitl
- One Grass, Ce malinalli
- One Lizard, Ce cuetzpalin
- One Vulture, Ce cozcacuauhtli
- One Rabbit, Ce tochtli

Belong to Mictlanpa, the place of rest and stillness, also called the place of the ancestors, because from there they guide us as long as we are willing to appreciate their advice. From Mictlanpa come the sense of reflection and a spirit of constant renewal. The arrangement of the directions in this new edition has now been corrected from the previous edition; we take the sequence that predominates in most codices, especially that of the Borgia Codex.

To know what kinds of energies are intimately related to each of us, it is necessary to gather all the calendrical elements and the forms of influence brought together on a particular date. These range from the influence dispersed throughout an entire year to the most personal, such as the date of birth with all the variations made known through these pages.
