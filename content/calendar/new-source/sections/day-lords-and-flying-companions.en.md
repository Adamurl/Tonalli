### Printed page 177

Of the thirteen Lords of the Days, called Tonaltecuhtin or Tonalteuhtin in the Mexican language, we shall only repeat the already familiar list so that it is included in this part, since in preceding pages we have devoted a detailed study to them because of their relationship with the thirteen numbers and the flying companions, including the elements that in the Tonalamatl of Aubin are emerging from each one's beak.

Let us return once again to the twenty day signs and the trecenas, looking for affinities. As we have observed in the parallel lists of the companions of the twenty day signs and the companions of the twenty trecenas, we observe correspondences because they are the same rulers or their equivalents up to the midpoint of the two lists, with the sign itzcuintli and the trecena ce tecpatl occupying tenth place, accompanied by Mictlantecuhtli. The ruler of the day is the same ruler of the trecena, sometimes accompanied by another element that regulates the first, as in the case of ce tecpatl, which is balanced by the strength of Tonatiuh, the Sun.

After the day ozomahtli, the correspondence is staggered, beginning with the sign malinalli, which is in twelfth place, and the eleventh trecena, which is ce ozomahtli. These particularities can be appreciated better in the table we shall develop later.

The day ozomahtli, whose companion is Xochipilli, is a special case that has no direct relationship, as the preceding signs do, with the trecena that occupies the same level in the sequence.
