## Full English translation

### Printed page 164

#### Mahtlactli Uan Ome

Matlactli uan ome is ten and two. In the Maya language it is *lahak*.

It is represented with two bars and two dots. In hieroglyphic writing there are two hands and two dots.

In the *tonalpohualli* it is the number of one who is acquiring inner beauty and luminosity, in essence and spirit, like the light of the Sun or the radiance of Tlahuizcalpantecuhtli, the Lord of Dawn. It is the shining beauty that emanates from within beings, which, according to Indigenous metaphors, *can manifest by letting the light transcend, passing through us*, so that everyone may see through a person. They are people who *leave no gaps in either their being or their actions; they do everything solidly*, because they say that *if they leave gaps, the air slips through them*.

Those who have the number twelve allow their purposes to be seen through their transparency, so as not to create doubts that cloud the clarity people must manifest in their thoughts and attitudes.

It is easy to suppose that what provides the qualities for this number is a concept forming part of the science of Quetzacoatl. The one who has clarity and luminosity for human beings is Tlahizcalpantecuhtli.

In the *tonalpohualli*, the flying companion of the number twelve is the *quetzaltototl*, a beautiful bird of brilliant blue-green tones whose symbolism is the same as jade's. They represent the constant search for the elevation and transparency of spirits in evolution.

Increasing the positive qualities of this entire set of characteristics, Centeotl emerges from the beautiful bird's beak in the *Tonalamatl de Aubin*.

**Matlactli uan ome.** The stage of experiences closes with the elevation of the inner part of our being. They are transparent, serene, and trustworthy people.

**Tlahuizcalpantecuhtli.** It is one's own luminosity, confidence, and light that guides other people.

**Quetzaltototl.** It represents those who rise above their human condition to attain inner beauty in essence and spirit.

**Centeotl.** It is creativity, sustenance, and sensitivity.

*Centeotl. Borgia Codex.*
