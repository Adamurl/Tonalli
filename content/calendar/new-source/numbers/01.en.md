## Full English translation

### Printed page 145

##### Ce

Ce, Cen, Cem. It is number one.

The Maya peoples of antiquity called number one hun.

Ce is the unit. Most analysts of the count of destiny agree that it means the beginning and origin that everything must have.

Xiuhuitzitzilli, companion of number one.

Cen, cem, is a whole made up of several elements, such as cenyeliztli, the family, cemilhuitl, a complete day, and cemanahuac, the world from the Mexica people's point of view.

Cemilhuitl, a complete day. Plate 11, Borbonicus.

This rule is not applied in the case of the twenty trecenas of a tonalpohualli. Let us remember that since there are only thirteen numbers, each group of thirteen days begins with numeral one. In this way, each trecena is distinguished from the others by naming it with one and the corresponding sign, for example: ce cipactli, ce ocelotl, ce mazatl, ce xochitl, and so on. Each trecena also has an accompanying element that influences it, imparting a special characteristic to the thirteen days forming the whole.

The nahuatlahto Luis Flores Torres states that number one, cen, represents the human being as a collection of attitudes, thoughts, and emotions forming an essence contained in the vessel that is the human body, whose name in Nahuatl is nacayotl. The body is the vessel that is itself made up of a collection of organs, some single, such as the heart and liver, but which together carry out a set of special functions in complete synchrony. The most complete account to date, in the Nahuatl language, of the parts of the human body was given by Alfredo López Austin in *Human Body and Ideology*.

Tlahuizcalpantecuhtli emerging from the white hummingbird's beak. Beside him is the daytime companion Xiuhtecuhtli. Tonalamatl of Aubin.

In the table of the numbers and their companions, the bird with number one is a hummingbird. It may be white, gray, or turquoise blue, depending on the codex in our hands. We favor blue, since the daytime companion among the thirteen lords is none other than Xiuhtecuhtli, whose color is turquoise blue with a very special meaning. Xiuhtecuhtli is the fundamental concept of time, the years, heat, and the energy that reaches it from above.

### Printed page 146

In people it represents justice, honor, uprightness, and fame, as we will see in more detail later, but everything is grounded in the centrality of Xiuhtecuhtli.

Blue is also the color of Huitzilopochtli, the left-handed hummingbird, who represents the capacity for decision and strength of will within a context of great activity. When these qualities are added together, the xiuhuitzizilin becomes the container of all of them, joined to number one, which, being at the beginning of the count, has meanings of origin, responsibility, leadership, and strength of will.

Xiuhtecuhtli with the blue hummingbird. Borbonicus Codex.

Emerging from the hummingbird's beak is the figure representing the morning star, the Lord of the Dawn called Tlahuizcalpantecuhtli, with all the luminosity and trust that farmers and early-rising travelers place in him as he guides them by lighting their way.

Bringing together all the characteristics available to us, the summary was arranged as follows, to be applied to a person in an attempt to understand through it the individual's way of developing in life.

Ce, one. Beginning, origin. Those born with this number are pioneers. In this number there is the capacity for leadership and responsibility to develop and achieve everything undertaken.

Cen, one whole. It represents working together in pursuit of common objectives, with ability, energy, and subtlety in bringing judgments together.

Xiuhuitzitzilli. Turquoise-blue hummingbird. It represents constant movement, an unshakable will for constant activity, beautiful presence, pleasant and appreciated people.

Xiuhtecuhtli. Centrality, a gift for justice, honorability, and uprightness. These are people who can be fully trusted. Because of their great responsibility they are an example for others to follow.

Tlahuizcalpantecuhtli. A luminosity of one's own, trust, light that serves as a guide for other people.
