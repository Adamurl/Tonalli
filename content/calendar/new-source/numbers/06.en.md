## Full English translation

### Printed page 155

#### Chicuace

Chicuace is the number six.

In the Maya language it is *uak*, and it is written with a bar and a dot above it. In the Mexican language it is *chicuace*. Literally it is the one, *ce*, that is placed before, *chicu*, to join those that preceded it. One finger of the other hand joins the open hand. The hieroglyph is drawn with an open hand accompanied by a dot or a small finger. The hand has the value of five, and the finger or dot has the value of one.

### Printed page 156

In nature it is the number that has structure and solidity.

The companion of the number six is Mictlantecuhtli, the Lord of Rest and Stillness, as Teoyaomiqui or Teoyamiqui, the essence of dead warriors. In people it is a subtle way of balancing impulsive attitudes, seeking states of spirituality through the transmuting characteristics of the Lord of Rest, Mictlantecuhtli.

The bird accompanying this number is Chicuahtli or *chicuauhtli*, the barn owl, which is sometimes exchanged for *tecolotl*, the owl, as a representative of introversion and wisdom. The meaning is the same in both cases.

In the *Tonalamatl de Aubin*, accompanying the barn owl is the face of Uauantli, the striped one, who represents a rebirth of the Earth through planted furrows and the sacrifice of the person who dedicates a lifetime to caring for the Earth.

Precisely because of the red and white striped paint, we know that Uauantli or Huahuantli forms part of the ordering principle contained in red Tezcatlipoca, made up of Xipe Totec, Mixcoatl, Iztapaltotec, and Camaxtli.

*Uahuantli and the barn owl. Tonalamatl de Aubin.*

The summary of characteristics gives us the interesting profile of a cautious person who acts reflectively, without haste, and with a defined objective:

**Chicuace.** Constant learning in search of spirituality.

**Mictlantecuhtli Teoyaomiqui.** Evolutionary and transcendent inner transformation in the learning stage, through the experiences one lives.

**Chicuahtli.** It represents constant, calm, and unperturbed evaluation. These are people who never act on impulse, who are reflective, patient, and centered. Intuition and intellect become active from the first hours of the night.

**Uauantli.** With striped body paint he represents work on the Earth, and within oneself, gradually transforming inwardly in search of order in the subconscious, carefully structuring ideas and attitudes so they can be projected outward.
