## Full English translation

### Printed page 151

#### Nahui

Nahui, number four. In the Maya language it is kan, serpent, vibrating energy.

Four is one of the principal factors of calendrical mathematics, involving most of nature's rhythms. From the Indigenous point of view, people nourish themselves with the tonalli they receive from the four directions of the universe, giving life-giving strength to their body and spirit. Physically, number four contains the explanation of the natural sequence in the formation of the human being.

Zolin, the quail.

The origin of its name in the Mexican language may derive from the verb nacahuia, which means to cover with flesh or to embody.

With this number, a person's primary formation is completed, with everything received from nature from each of the directions and with the basic parts that compose the person as a collection of elements, always acting in functions in harmony with each other. Let us remember that number one, ce, is the whole representing the human being, and from two to four there are both the supporting structure, omitl, and the liquid current that animates it, eztli, and in four, nacahuic, nahui, that which envelops and gives shape to each person. This way of perceiving human beings may seem somewhat naive, but in numerical language the formative situations of the human body and spirit become clear through the positional order of the pre-Hispanic mathematical system as an evolutionary record of each person's natural development and a commitment to growth on several levels.

Muscles covering the human skeleton.

Number four is the coming together of the four basic elements: heat, wind, and water upon the generating earth. Nahui are the first four Tezcatlipoca, children of Ometeotl: Yayauhqui, Tlatlauhqui, Huitzilopochtli, and Quetzalcoatl. In their cosmic function they metaphorically represent dark, distant, infinite time, Yayauhqui, in which ordered wholes become concrete and take coherent form, Tlatlauhqui, within which there is constant activity, Huitzilopochtli, that brings forth the precious thing that is life, Quetzalcoatl. They share the duality of Omeyocan, giving time, coherence, movement, and life to eternal space, where the dual essence exists.

Zolin. Borgia Codex.

### Printed page 152

Everyday life takes place mainly within the sphere of number four. A house has four walls, the ceremonial center has four extremities, every tzacualli and every momoztli has four sides, and the cultivated field where food is planted to sustain the family has four sides.

The ceremony to give a little boy his name must take place four days after birth or at four months of twenty days. His name is shouted four times because he is male, since men's cycles are governed by Tonatiuh, the Sun. With this ceremony, a first step is taken toward understanding his schematic universe and toward his confirmation as a human being.

Number four can be summarized as the result of the being's phase of primary integration. In other words, those who have numeral four possess a great capacity for organization, received through continually assimilating experiences of everything happening around them and adding them to their own experiences. The simplest diagram for drawing number four, apart from the common representation of successive dots, uses the same dots to form a square, in which what is necessary is metaphorically received from the influence of the universe's four directions for primary integration.

The bird that always accompanies number four is the quail, zolin, a little bird always linked with the earth because of its peculiarity of blending into it. Brown with lighter spots, it blends among the plants with the color of the earth, and often we come upon them on the hills or among the furrows, almost stepping on them without seeing them. It is common to feel their presence when they suddenly take flight. The quality represented by the quail is accumulating the Sun's heat in its body and remaining attached to earthly things, feeling and living this integration, behaving with the naturalness of someone who lives reality with feet firmly planted on the earth.

The daytime companion of number four is Tonatiuh, the one who carries and provides us with heat and light. He represents the qualities of being luminous, strong, a giver of life and inner warmth. These are trustworthy people, warm but energetic in manner, and luminous in appearance.

Tonatiuh. Plate 23, Borgia Codex.

From the quail's beak emerges Xipe Totec, who regulates the cyclicality of Mother Earth's changes of skin. In people these are qualities susceptible to nature's periodic changes, perceived in a constant regeneration that admits no possibility of stagnation within evolutionary and natural development.

### Printed page 153

The summary for number four was put together as follows.

Nahui, four. Integration. In this number are the qualities of knowing how to gather and collect everything we receive from nature, forming a balanced consciousness by making use of lived experiences.

Zolin. The quail. It represents the Sun's heat accumulated in the Earth. These are realistic people who live without evasions or fantasies. They are warm and affectionate. In most cases they go unnoticed, but when they are missing their absence is felt.

Tonatiuh. It is the Sun, the one who carries heat and light. In people this translates as affection, human warmth, confidence radiating as inner luminosity. Their manner is kind, although energetic.

Xipe Totec. Our Lord who changes skin. It is constant evolutionary transmutation.
