## Full English translation

### Printed page 119

#### Coatl

Coatl means serpent, and it is the generic name for reptiles such as boas, vipers, and snakes.

*Coatl. Plate 65, Borgia Codex.*

In the countryside it is said that:

> Snakes sense people and animals; they know where they are even in darkness; they say they sense with their foreheads; they put little animals to sleep when they stare at them; when they run, their whole body undulates; they feel with all their skin and shed it regularly; they say that if we learned truly to see, we would see the light coming out of trees, rocks, and people, undulating like little snakes.

With these simple opinions we can define the sign coatl as the manifestation of warm and luminous energy given off by the elements and by all beings that exist in nature, accepting thereby that it is a symbol of the precious energy of life. Whoever perceives it is a tlamatini whose perception is heightened, and therefore a wise man within true knowledge.

Coatl also represents Mother Earth's fertility cycles, which interweave their energy in the skirt of Tonantzin Coatlicue in mathematical cycles related to one another, generating the different species that live upon her.

The two-headed serpent, so familiar in drawings and sculptures, is the fusion of the two cosmic forces that generate everything in existence, opposing forces that at the same time complement each other so that space and time may exist. Within that context are matter, activity, and life.

The adornments in its representations are generally feathers, jewels, or rattles, depending on whether the reference is to the terrestrial, aquatic, aerial, or celestial.

Tezcatlipoca and Quetzalcoatl, in manifesting as pure energy, were represented as two great serpents. In the well-known narrative in André Thevet's book Hystoire du Mexique, the two great serpents divide Tlaltecuhtli, Lord of the Earth, in two, and with one of his parts form the terrestrial surface, identified as Oxomoco, and the atmospheric envelope as Cipactonal. In both, the heat coming from the great celestial Bowl accumulates, nourishing all the elements necessary for life to exist.

*Coatl. Stone of the Suns.*

### Printed page 120

Of those born on a day of the sign coatl, according to the tonalpohualli, it is said that they are very sensitive, generous people who do not like to hoard anything. They like to change their personal appearance or the appearance of their dwelling frequently. They have the ability to communicate through thought with those who vibrate at their same frequency. They can easily intensify their thoughts, concentrating to the point of influencing other people. The sensitivity of their skin helps them perceive atmospheric changes. They have such alert intelligence that for many people it is a symbol of wisdom.

The mentality of the European chroniclers, who saw the serpent as the Judeo-Christian symbol of original sin, led them to classify coatl as an evil sign.
