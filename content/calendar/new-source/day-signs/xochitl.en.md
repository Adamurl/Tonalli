## Full English translation

### Printed page 136

#### Xochitl

The xochitl sign is the last of the twenty day signs. It is at the end of the group, closing the cycle of each twenty-day period. The opinions gathered were simple but very eloquent regarding the meaning of the flower in the application of the tonalamatl.

> Xochitl is flower. Flowers are the most beautiful things we find in life. The countryside is more beautiful when it fills with flowers, because there are colors and fragrances that gladden the heart. Just seeing them makes one very happy and gladdens the heart, comparing them with beautiful women. We also compare songs and verses with flowers. When trees fill with flowers, it is a sign that there will be fruit. In life everything must flower. Even we flower by working, doing good things for the family and for the village. When we finish each job we do, it is as though we gave a flower.

*Xochitl. Plate 33, Bodley Codex.*

From all these expressions it follows that the flower represents the transcendent essence of the positive qualities that exist in each of us and throughout nature. Xochitl is everything beautiful and of quality. The message contained in the flower sign invites human beings to seek flowering thoughts and expressions, trying to direct all their activities toward full flowering in every aspect of life. This sign is also everything beautiful and flowering that the human mind can generate. People born under the flower sign are very creative and subtle in attaining an aim, which they achieve without haste and with the firm conviction that they will fulfill their commitments satisfactorily.

### Printed page 137

For these reasons we can affirm that xochitl represents everything that comes to a good conclusion, attaining the proposed goals with clear and defined objectives.

Xochitl is present in the sensitivity that gives rise to artistic creativity.

It is the ease of being able to express and share the feelings of the spirit through poetry, singing, music, dance, painting, and in general everything concerning art, brought together in the phrase in xochitl in cuicatl, flower and song.

Those who have a relationship with xochitl must be people clean in spirit, clear in speech, and with a willpower that withstands every test. As they say in the countryside, only in this way do they manage to flower, and those who flower bear excellent fruit.
