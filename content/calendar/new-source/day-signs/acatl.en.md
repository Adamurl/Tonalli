## Full English translation

### Printed page 127

#### Acatl

Acatl means reed. Like tule, it is a plant that grows abundantly in damp places. It is very useful in the countryside. The information gathered about the reed is as follows:

> Reeds are strong and very straight. We make flutes with them, and thin ones serve to make arrows. Thicker ones serve to make tlacochtli, spears one armspan long. Otate is like acatl, except that it is not hollow. With it, fire can be drawn from a soft little stick by spinning it rapidly on its tip with the hands.

*Acatl. Plate 12, Borgia Codex.*

Since the sixteenth century acatl has been wrongly translated as cane. In the Mexican language ohuatl means cane. Ohuatl are the jointed, solid, juicy stalks, such as those of maize and sugarcane.

Within the symbolism of the amoxtin, acatl represents the heat emanating from the Sun, called tonaacatl, whose function is to reach the earth with the fertilization necessary to produce sustenance.

### Printed page 128

In pre-Hispanic cosmogony, acatl is an important element as part of the calendrical names of the two concepts that form nature, called Ce-acatl, Quetzalcoatl, and Ome-acatl, Tezcatlipoca. These in turn, when demythologized, represent the bioenergy possessed by living beings, as organic energy and as the essence of the spirit. In the Mexica codices, acatl is represented by a clump of reeds sprouting from the bottom of a water channel, while in amoxtin of Mixtec tradition and style it is a bundle of reeds with arrowheads, depicting darts that, when thrown, convey the idea of speed, strength, direction, and consequently purposefulness.

In the hieroglyphs representing the lighting of a ceremonial fire, including the New Fire ceremony, as a bioenergetic cycle of Tezcatlipoca in Ome-acatl years, the reed is the vertical element that turns upon a horizontal piece of wood, which is ignited through friction. Acatl is generally adorned with eagle down or cotton tassels.

Those born on an acatl day have a very alert intelligence, eager to know and analyze in depth everything that interests them. They observe and analyze everything dispassionately. They always keep their objectives in mind. They are centered and not very emotional people, very frank in their dealings. They radiate confidence, although they are not very affectionate.

Fray Diego Durán says of those born on an acatl day:

> ...as the cane is hollow and without a heart, so those born under this sign were heartless men...

*Acatl. Stone of the Suns.*
