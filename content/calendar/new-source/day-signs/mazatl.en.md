## Full English translation

### Printed page 121

#### Mazatl

Mazatl is the generic name for the different species of deer that inhabit Mexico. Of mazatl it is said that:

> They are always attentive to what happens nearby; they are very nervous and have very keen hearing; they feel with their skin and are said to hear with their hooves; they run very fast through the brush and do not like to live in captivity.

*Mazatl. Plate 19, Borgia Codex.*

It is an animal of beautiful bearing, an agile and slender body, a restless nature, and great perceptiveness. With very keen hearing, it is always attentive to the sounds carried by the wind. It lives in the mountains, in constant relation to freedom in nature, becomes active with the Sun's energy, and is sensitive to changes in humidity brought by rain.

In symbolism, mazatl is related to the Sun, heat, and fire. It is therefore identified as a solar animal, as are some birds and certain insects, such as the hummingbird and butterfly. In the Borgia Codex it appears on plate 53, representing Xochipilli as the Sun's fertilizing energy, with the twenty day signs and a butterfly drawn on its face.

Durán says of the sign mazatl:

> ...that those born under it were restless and never stayed in one place.

Sahagún says only that it is a good sign, without going into detail. In the countryside they still say that those whose attitudes are related to deer are nervous and restless and always attentive to what happens around them. They have good hearing and are sensitive to changes in the weather, such as heat, wind, and the moisture of rain. They like open spaces and freedom and prefer to live in the countryside, since in cities they feel imprisoned. They are very affectionate, sometimes even sacrificing themselves for those they love.

### Printed page 122

They have an agile, slender physique and a harmonious, elegant appearance. They are very noble and like to run in the forests and mountains, enjoying the feeling of rain and wind moving over their skin.

*Mazatl. Stone of the Suns.*
