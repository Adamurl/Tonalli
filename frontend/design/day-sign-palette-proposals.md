# Pastel palette proposals for the twenty day signs

These are modern visual directions for a later pass. They are not historical color assignments. The current reference section keeps its red palette.

Use the light color as the panel background, the dark ink for headings and body copy, and the accent for decorative artwork. Keep body text at full opacity. White navigation works over the current red section and dark imagery; a pale panel needs the dark ink for readable navigation.
